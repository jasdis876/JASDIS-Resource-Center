Gia salary period correction

- 2025/26 is the CURRENT payroll baseline.
- The 2026/27 +5% rate is NOT treated as already applied to the payslip.
- 2026/27 is shown as the revised rate effective 1 April 2026.
- 2027/28 is shown as the further +5% rate effective 1 April 2027.
- Current payslip Basic Pay is matched only against the 2025/26 scale.
- Retro is the difference between 2025/26 and 2026/27. If implementation starts in November 2026, April-October is shown as 7 months.
- Take-home estimates can be viewed separately for each of the three periods.
